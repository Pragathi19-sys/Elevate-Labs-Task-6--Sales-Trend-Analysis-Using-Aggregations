Task 6 - Sales Trend Analysis

Objective:
To find out how much monthly revenue was generated and how many monthly orders were received from the online sales data.

About the Dataset:
This is a dataset with 240 orders. It has data from January 2024 to August 2024. Each order contains date, amount, and product details.

What I Did:
1. Separated month from the date
2. Calculated total revenue sum for each month
3. Counted how many orders came for each month
4. Arranged months in time order

Results:
Highest sales were in January and lowest sales were in July. Sales were high in the starting months and decreased in the middle.

Skills Learned:
Learned how to group month-wise data, calculate revenue, and analyze sales trends.
